Each and every day, we should aim to challenge our brain with exercises to not only learn and stay current but to ultimately stay healthy. Engaging the brain consistently has shown to slow cognitive decline and reduce the risk of dementia. That can be tough at the end of a long day, as we’re often tired and want to turn off our brain.
